Play a piece of music

Import a MusicXML file (.musicxml, .mxl or .xml) and play through it note by note.

  1. Already in a notation app? MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale, Dorico, Guitar Pro: File → Export → MusicXML.
  2. Got a PDF or a photo? Scan it into MusicXML first, with MuseScore’s free PDF import (musescore.com/import), Audiveris, or an app such as PlayScore 2. Check the result in MuseScore, then export MusicXML.
  3. Looking for music? Many score sites offer MusicXML downloads.

Fretlab follows the top line of the music, chooses where to play each note on the neck, and ignores repeats for now.
